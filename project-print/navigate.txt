Solar Potential Predictor documentation
=======================================

Start with technical-blueprint.md. It is the primary technical research report:
it states the tract-level research question, documents the data and feature
representation, describes the reproducible modeling workflow, and records the
evaluation and interpretation boundaries.

Use project-overview.txt for a shorter research-oriented summary of the study and
its repository map.

Use debugging-notes.txt for operational troubleshooting only. It explains how to
rebuild processed artifacts and preserves the distinction between offline model
evaluation and the Streamlit demonstration interface.

agent-planning.md is an internal implementation handoff. It is useful when
changing code, but it is not the project’s research narrative.
